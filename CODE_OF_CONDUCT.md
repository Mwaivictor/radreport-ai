# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project brings together clinicians, engineers, researchers and students, in several countries and at different stages of training. Treat everyone with respect.

We expect people to be honest about evidence, to disagree about ideas and not about people, to credit others' work, and to respect local expertise, particularly from African colleagues.

We do not accept harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sharing someone's private information, or pressure to overstate results.

## Patients come first

Treat patients and their data with the respect you would give them in clinic. Never mock, expose or try to identify anyone.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports are handled in confidence. Unacceptable behaviour may lead to removed contributions or a ban.
